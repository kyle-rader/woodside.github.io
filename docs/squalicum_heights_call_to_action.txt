Subject: URGENT: Submit Comments on Squalicum Heights Development by May 8

Dear Woodside Neighbors,

We need your help to protect our neighborhood. The deadline to submit comments about the Squalicum Heights development is May 8 at 5:00 PM.

HOW TO COMMENT:
- Email: squalicumheights@cob.org
- You must include your name and mailing address

KEY CONCERNS TO CONSIDER:
- Traffic Impact: Increased daily trips through Pebble Place and Woodside Way
- Safety: Single entrance/exit point for 45 new homes, including emergency vehicles
- Neighborhood Character: Town-homes vs. single-family home character
- Parking: Limited parking spaces for visitors and potential overflow into our neighborhood.

Your voice matters! The more detailed comments the city receives, the better chance we have of being heard. 

Submit your comments by May 8!

Best regards,
Woodside Community Association (WCA) Board
