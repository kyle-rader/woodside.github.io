**Proposed Fine Schedule:**
• First violation: $50
• Second violation: $150
• Continuing violation: $100 per week

**Tree & View Rules:**
• 45 days to correct issues
• Weekly fines begin after 45-day correction period
• HOA may hire contractor after notice + hearing if violations are not corrected

**Homeowner Rights:**
• Written notice of violations
• Right to request a hearing
• Written decision following any hearing

**Legal Authority:**
The Board has full legal authority to adopt this fine schedule after proper notice. This authority comes from:
• Bylaws (Article 13): Board may adopt rules including a fine structure and levy fines after notice + hearing
• State Law (RCW 64.38.020(11)): Board may adopt enforcement procedures
• State Law (RCW 64.38.030): No owner vote required unless documents say otherwise
• CC&Rs are silent on fines and do not require owner approval
