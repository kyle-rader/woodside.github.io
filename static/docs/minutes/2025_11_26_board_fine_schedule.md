HOA Homeowner Packet: Fine Schedule & Enforcement Policy

Why No Homeowner Vote Is Required
	•	Washington law allows the Board to adopt rules and fines.
	•	CC&Rs do not mention fines or require owner approval.
	•	Bylaws explicitly give the Board authority to adopt a fine structure.

Fine Schedule
	•	First violation: $50
	•	Second violation: $150
	•	Continuing violation: $100 per week

Tree & View Rules
	•	45 days to correct issues
	•	Weekly fines begin after 45 days
	•	HOA may hire contractor after notice + hearing

Homeowner Rights
	•	Written notice
	•	Right to request hearing
	•	Written decision

This policy ensures fairness, consistency, and enforcement of our Covenants.

Board-Only Legal Briefing: Authority to Adopt Fine Schedule

1. CC&Rs
	•	Silent on fines.
	•	No owner vote requirement.

2. Bylaws (Article 13)
	•	Board may adopt rules including a fine structure.
	•	Board may levy fines after notice + hearing.

3. State Law
	•	RCW 64.38.020(11) — Board may adopt enforcement procedures.
	•	RCW 64.38.030 — No owner vote required unless documents say otherwise.

4. Requires Owner Vote
	•	Amendments to CC&Rs or Bylaws.

5. Does NOT Require Owner Vote
	•	Rules
	•	Fine schedules
	•	Enforcement procedures

Conclusion

The Board has full legal authority to adopt the fine schedule after proper notice.
